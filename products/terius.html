Terius: your questions

Do I need a licence to ride the Terius?

No. The Terius is a low-speed electric scooter, so you can ride it without a driving licence, RTO registration or number plate.

How far does the Terius go on one charge?

Over 120km with a lithium-ion battery and over 80km with lead-acid, depending on rider weight and road conditions.

Where can I see the Terius in Kasaragod?

At our Ronz EV showrooms in Chengala (Vadhi Thoybha, 671541) and Melparamba (Bridgestone – The Tyre Shop, KSTP Road, 671317), where you can take a free test ride.

What colours is the Terius available in?

Black, Grey, White, Red.
