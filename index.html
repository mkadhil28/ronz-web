No licence?
No problem.

Everything you need to know about riding a low-speed electric scooter without a driving licence or registration in Kasaragod and across Kerala.

Do I need a driving licence to ride a Ronz EV scooter?

No. Ronz EV low-speed models have a top speed of up to 25 km/h and a motor rated up to 250 W. Under Indian motor vehicle rules, electric two-wheelers within these limits are exempt from a driving licence.

Does a no licence electric scooter need registration or a number plate?

Low-speed electric scooters within the 25 km/h and 250 W limits do not need RTO registration or a number plate, so you can start riding as soon as you buy.

Who can ride a no licence scooter?

They are ideal for students, college-goers, seniors, homemakers, and anyone making short daily trips who does not hold a licence or does not want the paperwork.

What is the range of a Ronz EV scooter?

Lithium-ion models deliver more than 120 km on a single charge, and lead-acid models more than 80 km, depending on rider weight and road conditions.

How long does it take to charge?

A lithium-ion battery charges fully in about 3 to 4 hours and a lead-acid battery in about 4 to 5 hours, from a regular home socket.

Where can I buy a no licence electric scooter in Kasaragod?

Ronz EV has two showrooms in Kasaragod district: Vadhi Thoybha, Chengala (671541) and inside Bridgestone – The Tyre Shop on KSTP Road, Melparamba (671317). Both sell low-speed electric scooters you can ride without a licence or registration.
