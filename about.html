About Ronz EV

Freedom to ride, no licence needed

Ronz EV is an electric scooter dealer in Kasaragod, Kerala. We sell low-speed electric scooters that anyone can ride without a driving licence or registration, from our showrooms in Chengala and Melparamba.

Getting around shouldn't depend on a licence test, RTO paperwork or rising fuel prices. That's why Ronz EV focuses on low-speed electric scooters: a simple, affordable way for students, seniors and families to make everyday trips on their own.

Every model we stock has a top speed of up to 25 km/h and a motor up to 250 W, the limits for riding without a licence in India. They charge from a regular home socket and go up to 120 km on a charge.

What we offer

Only no licence models

Every scooter we sell is a low-speed electric model you can ride without a driving licence, RTO registration or number plate.

Free test rides

Try any model at our Chengala or Melparamba showroom before you buy, with our team there to answer every question.

Battery warranty

Lithium-ion and lead-acid battery options, backed by an 18-month battery warranty for peace of mind.

Local service & support

Help with charging, maintenance and repairs close to home, so your scooter stays on the road.

Who rides a Ronz EV

Built for everyday people

  • Students heading to school, college or tuition
  • Seniors who want easy, independent travel
  • Homemakers running daily errands and market trips
  • Workers commuting short distances every day
  • Anyone without a licence, or who wants to skip the paperwork
